# HoneyBird Community Guidelines

HoneyBird is a sanctuary for food lovers. Our goal is to create a
respectful space where people can celebrate food, cooking, and culinary
culture from around the world.

These guidelines explain the behavior expected from all users.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Keep Content Food Related

HoneyBird exists to celebrate food.

Acceptable content includes:

-   Food photography
-   Recipes
-   Restaurant reviews
-   Cooking tips
-   Culinary stories
-   Cultural food traditions

Content unrelated to food may be removed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Be Respectful

HoneyBird welcomes people of all cultures, religions, and backgrounds.

Users must treat others with respect and kindness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. No Harassment or Bullying

Do not:

-   Insult or attack other users
-   Threaten people
-   Harass users through comments or messages

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. No Hate Speech or Discrimination

Content that promotes discrimination against people based on:

-   Race
-   Religion
-   Gender
-   Culture
-   Nationality
-   Sexual orientation
-   Disability

is strictly prohibited.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. No Explicit Content

Do not post:

-   Sexual content
-   Pornography
-   Graphic violence
-   Disturbing imagery

HoneyBird is meant to be safe for a wide audience.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Respect Food Cultures

Food is deeply connected to culture and tradition.

Users should celebrate culinary diversity and avoid mocking or
disrespecting other cultures' food practices.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Report Problems

If you see content that violates these guidelines, report it through the
app or contact support.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Enforcement

HoneyBird may remove content or restrict accounts that violate these
guidelines to maintain a safe and respectful community.
