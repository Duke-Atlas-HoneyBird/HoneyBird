# HoneyBird Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HoneyBird collects, uses, and protects
user information when using the platform.

This policy is designed to comply with the **Protection of Personal
Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa** where applicable.

Website: https://duke-home.vercel.app\
Support Email: hdesk.honeybird@gmail.com

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

-   Username
-   Email address
-   Profile information
-   Posts, blogs, photos, and captions
-   Comments
-   Messages sent to other users

### Automatically Collected Information

-   Device information
-   IP address
-   Usage activity
-   App performance data

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

-   Operate and maintain the platform
-   Display user content in the Feed
-   Enable messaging
-   Improve platform performance
-   Detect abuse or violations
-   Ensure community safety

We do **not sell personal data to third parties**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Advertising

HoneyBird plans to support the platform through advertising.

This may include:

-   Display ads within the Feed
-   Sponsored content
-   Targeted advertisements based on general platform activity

Third-party advertising partners may use limited anonymous data such as
device identifiers or interaction data to deliver relevant ads.

HoneyBird does not share sensitive personal information with
advertisers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. User Content

Content posted on HoneyBird may be visible to other users depending on
visibility settings.

Users should avoid posting personal or sensitive information publicly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Messaging Privacy

Direct messages are intended to be private.

However, HoneyBird may review messages when:

-   Abuse or harassment is reported
-   Safety concerns arise
-   Platform rules are violated

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Data Security

HoneyBird uses reasonable safeguards to protect user data from:

-   Unauthorized access
-   Misuse
-   Loss

However, no digital platform can guarantee complete security.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Data Retention

User information is retained only as long as necessary to:

-   Operate the service
-   Comply with legal obligations
-   Resolve disputes
-   Enforce platform policies

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Children's Privacy

HoneyBird is not intended for children under the age of 13.

If such data is discovered, it will be removed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Policy Updates

HoneyBird may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updated versions
will be posted with a new effective date.
